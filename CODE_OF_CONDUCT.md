# Code of Conduct

## NEXORA Student Chapter – VIIT

NEXORA Student Chapter – VIIT is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and encouraging technical environment for all students at Vishwakarma Institute of Information Technology. Whether you are a beginner writing your first line of code or an experienced developer, we expect all members, mentors, and contributors to foster a supportive learning space.

---

## 1. Core Principles

### Respectful Communication
- Use welcoming and inclusive language in issue discussions, pull request comments, and chat channels.
- Treat every member with empathy and respect, regardless of technical background or experience level.

### Constructive Feedback
- Code reviews must be educational and encouraging. Focus feedback on code structure, readability, and logic—never on personal skill level.
- Frame suggestions constructively (e.g., *"Here's an alternative approach that might improve performance..."* rather than *"This code is bad"*).

### Collaboration & Peer Growth
- Support fellow students in learning Git, GitHub, and programming concepts.
- Share knowledge freely, answer questions patiently, and celebrate each other's technical achievements.

---

## 2. Unacceptable Behavior

We strictly prohibit behavior that creates an uncomfortable or unsafe environment, including:

- **No Harassment**: Personal attacks, insult-driven arguments, or inappropriate comments are not tolerated.
- **No Discrimination**: Discrimination based on gender, identity, race, ethnicity, age, disability, academic performance, or background will result in immediate community action.
- **No Trolling or Disruption**: Sustained disruption of discussions or intentional submission of malicious code/spam PRs.

---

## 3. Academic Integrity & Intellectual Property

As a college technical community, academic honesty and legal compliance are essential:

- **Academic Integrity**: Do not use NEXORA repositories to plagiarize coursework, violate college exam rules, or share unauthorized solution keys.
- **Proper Attribution**: Always credit original authors, open-source libraries, and references when utilizing third-party code.
- **Respect for Intellectual Property**: Only commit code, images, and data that you have created or have explicit legal rights/licenses to share.
- **Responsible Use of Repositories**: Use organization resources solely for educational, project development, and chapter-approved activities.

---

## 4. Enforcement & Reporting

The `technical-team` and faculty advisors are responsible for upholding these community standards:

- If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to a `technical-team` lead or chapter mentor.
- All reports will be handled confidentially and investigated promptly.
- Violations may result in warnings, temporary restriction from organization repositories, or escalation to college chapter leadership.

---

## 5. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces managed by **NEXORA Student Chapter – VIIT**, including GitHub repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, workshops, hackathons, and associated communication channels.
